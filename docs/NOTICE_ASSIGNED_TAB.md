# NOTICE — Your new "Assigned" list is live

**To:** All CS · Teams A, B and C · **From:** Aldo · **Date:** 11 August 2026 · **Status:** Live now

---

## What changed

You no longer have to hunt for who to approach. Open **Daily → Assigned** and your list is already there, ready, ranked from most important to least.

Before, we each picked our own players. Some of us worked twice as hard for half the score, simply because we were picking the wrong names. That stops today.

## Why we built it

Two honest reasons:

1. **Fairness.** Everyone now gets the same mix — the same share of whales, the same share of the high-scoring players. Nobody gets the easy pile, nobody gets the dead pile.
2. **Your KPI.** The system puts the best-scoring players at the top of your list. Work top to bottom and you are working the highest-value names first, every single day.

## Who gets it

**Every agent, on every team.** Log in, go to Daily, click **Assigned**. Your own list, your own names.

You only ever see **your own team's players**, and only your own share of them. You won't see another agent's pile, and you won't see another team's. Managers can see all of us — that's it.

## What's on the list

Only players who are genuinely workable. Three hard rules:

- **They have a Telegram link.** If we can't reach them, they're not on your list. No wasted effort.
- **They've been quiet 2+ days.** We don't disturb active players.
- **Maximum 30 per person, per day** — and you can open another batch of 30 when you clear it. It is meant to be finishable.

## How to read it

| Tag | What it means | How to treat it |
|---|---|---|
| **COOLING** | A whale gone quiet 5–6 days | Drop everything. This is the last window before we lose them. Personal message. |
| **WHALE** | A top-value player quiet 7+ days | High worth. Worth your best effort. |
| **REACTIVATE** | Idle 7–29 days | This is our money zone — these score the most on the reactivation KPI. |
| **WATCH** | Idle 2–6 days | Light, friendly check-in. |
| **CHURN** | Idle 30+ days | Lowest priority. Soft touch only, don't burn time here. |

**Three numbers, don't mix them up:**

- **Value** = how much the player is **down to us**. Bigger number, more worth keeping. If you see a **+** and the number is greyed out, that player is **up on us** — still worth a polite touch, but the lowest value on your list.
- **Quiet** = plain days since their last deposit.
- **Risk %** = how likely they are to leave us, based on *their own* deposit rhythm. A player who normally deposits every 2 days and has been quiet 6 is in real danger. A player who normally takes 10 days between deposits and has been quiet 6 is fine. Same "Quiet 6d", very different risk.
  - **A dash (—) means we don't know their rhythm yet.** Most players will show a dash, and that is on purpose — we would rather show you nothing than a made-up number. When you see a dash, go by **Value** and **Quiet**.

## How to work it

1. Start at the top. The order is already the right order — don't jump around.
2. Click **Message** to open their Telegram straight away.
3. Click the **row** to open the full card — who they are, their history, and a suggested approach. Write it in your own words, never copy-paste.
4. **Log it. Every time.** Two ways, both count the same:
   - The **✓** button on the row — one click, logs it as contacted under your name. Use this for a plain send.
   - The **full card** — use this when you want to add a note or set a proper outcome later.

   A player you messaged but never logged does not count — not for you, not for the team. Unlogged work is invisible work.
5. Once someone is logged, the button turns into **Chat** so you can still reopen their Telegram for a follow-up.
6. Watch the "done · to go" counter at the top. Clear it.

## The rhythm

After you contact someone, they **rest 3 days**. If they're still quiet after that, they come back to your list. If they ignore us **3 times**, we park them and give you someone winnable instead. That's by design — we chase value, not ghosts.

## Two things to keep in mind

- **The data is one day behind.** Yesterday's deposits show up today. So keep your messages as warm check-ins, never "you haven't deposited" — if they already came back, a check-in still lands well and a demand looks foolish.
- **Quiet on this list already counts that extra day**, so it can read one day higher than the same player's DSLD elsewhere in the console. That is on purpose — this list is aged to the truth, not to the file.
- **Never follow a loss with a deposit request.** That rule hasn't changed and it never will.
