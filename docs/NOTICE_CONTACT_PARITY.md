# NOTICE — Assigned list update: fresh names first

**To:** All CS · Teams A, B and C · **From:** Aldo · **Date:** 11 September 2026 · **Status:** Live now

---

## The one thing to know

Look for the green **NEW** badge.

A player counts toward our September target **once a month**. First message only. If you message someone a second time, it does nothing for the score — the badge tells you which is which, at a glance.

Follow-ups are still fine when a conversation is live. Just don't spend the day on names we already counted.

## What changed

- **Your list leads with players nobody has contacted this month.** They sit above everyone else, whatever their value.
- **Once those run out, the list rotates** — oldest contact first, so we work through everyone instead of circling the same familiar names.
- **Your cap is now 30 a day**, up from 15. Cleared it and still have shift? Hit **+ Open next batch (30)**. Up to 4 batches.
- **New counter at the top of Assigned** shows where the team stands, what we need per day, and how many you've added today.
- **⚠ on a row** means another agent already messaged that player this week. Skip it unless you have a reason.

## Why

Through the first ten days of September, **43% of our messages went to people we had already contacted** — 364 out of 853. Meanwhile 693 reachable players had never been messaged at all. Nobody was slacking; the old list simply kept handing back the same names.

## The number we're chasing

**52% of our reachable players contacted, by 30 September.** We're at 41.3%.

That's **127 more people**, about **7 a day**. Easily inside what we already do — we just have to point it at new names.

Clear 52% and the next mark opens on its own: 60, then 70, and up. No stopping at the line.

## What we expect

Work your list top to bottom. Green badges first. If you clear it, open the next batch. If you see the ⚠, leave it for the agent who already has the conversation.

That's it. Same effort, aimed better.
