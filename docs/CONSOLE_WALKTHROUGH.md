# VIP Retention Console — Quick Walkthrough

Hi team! Here's a simple explainer on our console — **what it is and how it works** — laid out in 5W1H. For the full step-by-step on actually working the queue, see the **Agent Handbook**.

---

## WHAT — what is the console?

It's our **daily VIP-retention system.** It takes every high-roller's activity across all our brands and turns it into one thing: a clear list of **who to look after today.**

For each player it tells us three things — is this player at risk, should we contact them or leave them alone, and what's the right thing to say. It does this across **21 brands** and **3 teams (A, B, C)**.

---

## WHY — why does it exist?

Our high rollers bring in a big chunk of our revenue, and keeping them is much cheaper than always chasing new ones.

Before this, retention was guesswork spread across messy spreadsheets. The console replaces all that with **one smart, consistent system** that spots the players who are slipping and keeps us from spamming the happy ones. **Winning together, growing forever.**

---

## WHO — who is it for?

Our **CS agents**, split into **Teams A, B, and C.** Each person logs in with their own account and sees **only their own team's brands** — so everyone works a clean, focused list. Managers and admins sit on top to run accounts and watch the numbers.

*(Need help? Login and accounts → your manager/admin. Something looks broken → the tech owner.)*

---

## WHEN — when does it work?

**Every day.** Fresh data comes in around **3 PM Malaysia time**, and that becomes the day's list.

It's built for **same-day action** — a tier deadline or a big loss today can't wait until tomorrow, so the console always works off today's picture.

---

## WHERE — where does it live?

It's a **website** — opened in the browser on a desktop. **Link:** `[insert console link]`

Everything sits in five tabs:
- **Dashboard** — the day at a glance
- **Daily** — the action list (the heart of it)
- **Changes** — who moved since yesterday
- **Performance** — how we're scoring
- **Settings** — accounts and password

---

## HOW — how does it work?

Simple idea under the hood:

1. **It pulls the numbers.** Every day it reads each player's activity from the source sheets.
2. **It sorts every player by behaviour** — about to lose their tier, big loss today, gone quiet, still healthy, and so on.
3. **It hands us a ready-made action list**, ordered by urgency, with a suggested way in for each player.
4. **It tracks what we did** — who we contacted, who came back — and scores how well we're saving players.

The one rule it's built around: **contact the players slipping away, leave the happy ones alone** — and remember a player who's losing is one of our most valuable.

---

## What we expect from this console

At the end of the day, here's what we're aiming for:

- **Nobody slips through.** Every at-risk VIP gets the right attention, on time.
- **More players saved and brought back.** A higher save rate, month after month.
- **Smarter, personal contact — not spam.** Our VIPs should feel looked after, not chased.
- **One consistent way of working** across all teams, so we grow steadily as we add more brands.
- **And the big one:** we keep our best players happy and playing.

**Winning together, growing forever.** Let's make it count.
