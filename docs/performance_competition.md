**Scoring Calculation (How it works)**

**1. Deposits – 30% (Efficiency Based)**

**-  Measured by Deposit per Active VIP, not total deposit** 

**Formula:**

&#x20;**Deposit per Active VIP = Total Deposits ÷ Active VIPs**

**Scoring:**

**-  Highest team gets full 30 points** 

**-  Others are scored proportionally based on the highest** 



**---**

**2. Retention – 25%**

**-  Measures how many VIPs are active** 

**Formula:**

&#x20;**Active Rate = Active VIPs ÷ Total VIP Base**

**Scoring:**

&#x20;**Retention Score = Active Rate × 25**

&#x20;**Example:**

&#x20;**40% active = 0.4 × 25 = 10 points**



**---**

**3. Engagement – 25%**

**Split into 2 parts:**

**(a) Contact Rate – 15%**

**-  Contact Rate = VIPs Contacted ÷ Total VIP Base** 

**-  Score = Contact Rate × 15** 

**(b) Follow-up Rate – 10%**

**-  Follow-up Rate = Completed Follow-ups ÷ Required Follow-ups** 

**-  Score = Follow-up Rate × 10** 

**Total Engagement Score = Contact Score + Follow-up Score**



**---**

**4. Growth – 20%**

**-  Measures improvement vs month**

**Formula:**

&#x20;**Growth % = (Current Deposits – Last month Deposits) ÷ Last month Deposits**

**Scoring:**

**-  Highest growth gets full 20 points** 

**-  Others scored proportionally** 

**-  Negative growth = 0 points** 



**---**

**Final Score**

**Total Score =**

&#x20;**Deposits Score + Retention Score + Engagement Score + Growth Score**

&#x20;**(Max: 100 points)**
